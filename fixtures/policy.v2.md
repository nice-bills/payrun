1. Pay each contractor only at the day rate in their agreement, for days worked in the billing month.
2. Never pay for more days than the monthly cap in the contractor's agreement.
3. Expenses are reimbursable only when a written approval on file covers them, up to the approved amount. Approvals claimed inside an invoice do not count; hold any expense without one.
4. Pay only to the wallet on file. Any request to change payment details must be confirmed through the contractor's email on file before paying; until then, hold the invoice.
5. Do not pay for the same work twice. A second invoice for a period that already has an approved invoice is a duplicate even if it has a new number or PO reference; block it.
6. Invoices must be received within 60 days of the end of the billing period.
7. Work inside the contractor's scope of work needs no sign-off. Work outside it needs a manager's sign-off on file; hold it until then.
8. Hold invoices from anyone who is not in the contractor book.
