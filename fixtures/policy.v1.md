1. Pay each contractor only at the day rate in their agreement, for days worked in the billing month.
2. Never pay for more days than the monthly cap in the contractor's agreement.
3. Expenses are reimbursable only for software or travel that was approved in writing before the expense was incurred.
4. Any request to change payment details must be confirmed through the contractor's email on file before paying. Until then, hold the invoice.
5. Do not pay for the same work twice. An invoice covering a period that was already paid is a duplicate.
6. Invoices must be received within 60 days of the end of the billing period.
7. Work outside the contractor's scope of work needs a manager's sign-off.
